What is SPYRIX Invoice?

A browser-based workspace for invoices, customer records, products, payments and reports.

Can I make GST invoices?

Yes. Layouts include GSTIN fields and configurable tax rates, shown in the preview and exported document.

Payments and stock?

Yes. Record full or partial payments, see outstanding balances and watch low-stock items.
